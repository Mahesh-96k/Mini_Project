Understanding Blood Pressure

Know what the two numbers represent.

Systolic Pressure

The upper number represents the pressure in your arteries when your heart beats.


Diastolic Pressure

The lower number represents the pressure in your arteries when your heart rests between beats.


Blood pressure interpretation depends on repeated measurements and individual medical circumstances. These comparisons are for tracking only and do not provide a diagnosis.
