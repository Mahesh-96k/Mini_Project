Understanding Body Temperature

Basic information about temperature readings.

What is body temperature?

Body temperature indicates how warm your body is. It can vary depending on measurement location, time of day, age and individual factors.


Understanding readings

Around 37°C is often used as a typical reference for adult body temperature, but normal readings vary between individuals and measurement methods.


A single temperature reading cannot establish a diagnosis. Follow appropriate medical advice if you are concerned about your reading or symptoms.
