Understanding Heart Rate

Learn what BPM means.

What is Heart Rate?

Heart rate is the number of times your heart beats in one minute. It is measured in BPM (Beats Per Minute).


What affects Heart Rate?

Physical activity, sleep, stress, emotions, illness and medications can affect heart rate.


Heart rate varies with age, activity and health. A change between two readings alone does not establish whether your health is improving or worsening. This page is for personal tracking, not diagnosis.
