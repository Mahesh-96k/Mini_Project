Blood Sugar Reference Guide

Common diagnostic reference ranges for non-pregnant adults.

Measurement Normal Prediabetes Diabetes threshold
Fasting Blood Sugar 70–99 mg/dL 100–125 mg/dL ≥126 mg/dL
2-hour OGTT Below 140 mg/dL 140–199 mg/dL ≥200 mg/dL
HbA1c Below 5.7% 5.7–6.4% ≥6.5%
Random Blood Sugar No single cutoff Not defined ≥200 mg/dL with classic symptoms

Important things to remember

• Below 70 mg/dL is generally considered low blood sugar.

• A normal fasting result does not guarantee that HbA1c or other glucose tests are normal.

• A single abnormal result does not automatically establish a diabetes diagnosis. Clinical confirmation may be required.

• Random and ordinary post-meal readings require interpretation based on timing, symptoms and context.

• Pregnancy and individual medical circumstances may require different reference ranges.

This guide is for educational purposes only. It does not determine whether a person is safe, diagnose diabetes or replace medical advice.
