Understanding Weight Tracking

Why consistent measurements matter.

Track consistently

Try to measure weight under similar conditions, such as at the same time of day and using the same weighing scale.


Understand changes

Weight can fluctuate due to hydration, food, activity and other factors. A single change does not necessarily indicate a health problem.


Weight history is for personal tracking. Weight change alone does not establish whether a person is healthy or unhealthy.
