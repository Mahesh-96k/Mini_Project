Understanding Sleep Tracking

Why monitoring sleep duration matters.

Why track sleep?

Sleep is important for physical health, mental well-being and daily functioning.


Recommended Sleep Duration

Adults generally need at least 7 hours of sleep per night, although individual needs vary.


Sleep duration alone does not measure sleep quality or diagnose a sleep disorder.
